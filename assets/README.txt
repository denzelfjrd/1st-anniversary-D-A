========================================================================
HAPPY ANNIVERSARY APP - ASSETS DIRECTORY
========================================================================

Place your media files in these folders:

1. assets/images/
   - Place your PNG, JPG, JPEG, GIF, or WebP photos here.
   - Example: assets/images/our_first_date.jpg

2. assets/videos/
   - Place your MP4, WebM, or MOV video clips here.
   - Example: assets/videos/sweet_moments.mp4

3. assets/music/
   - Place your MP3 audio files here for the background music player.
   - Example: assets/music/track1.mp3, assets/music/track2.mp3

Refer to walkthrough.md for full instructions on how to link these files in script.js!
